Artificial intelligence, or AI, refers to computer systems designed to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding language, and recognizing images or speech. AI is used in many areas, including healthcare, education, transportation, entertainment, and customer service. Through machine learning, AI systems can analyze large amounts of data and improve their performance over time. Although AI offers many benefits, it also raises important concerns about privacy, bias, employment, accuracy, and responsible use.
